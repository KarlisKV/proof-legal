Privacy Policy

What stays on your iPhone, what leaves it, and how you control your record.

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Version 1.0

Who we are

Proof is published by RVS Commerce, SIA, registration number 40203766164, Brīvības iela 40-27, Rīga, LV-1050, Latvia ("we", "us"). We are responsible for personal data we receive through support and operation of our services. Contact: admin@ecomagain.com.

Your record stays on your iPhone

Proof has no account or developer-operated cloud record storage. Entries, names of children and other people, contact details, emails you identify as yours, reminder dates, location labels, photos, audio, video, receipts, imported messages and generated exports are stored in the app on your iPhone. We do not receive the contents of that record through normal use of Proof.

Text recognition, screenshot ordering and available speech transcription run on the device. Proof does not upload your original messages or recordings to an AI service. It does not contact the co-parent.

Public timestamps

When Proof seals entries or signs an export, it requests an RFC 3161 timestamp from DigiCert. The request contains a SHA-256 fingerprint and a random request identifier, not your names, entry text, screenshots or original files. The provider receives connection information such as your IP address and request time. The current timestamp connection uses HTTP; request fingerprints and connection metadata can be visible on the network. Proof validates the signed response before calling a timestamp verified.

This is an external timestamp service, not publication of your record. See DigiCert's Privacy Center. If no connection is available, entries remain sealed locally and wait for a timestamp.

Permissions and Apple services

Camera, microphone, selected photos, files, location, speech recognition and notifications are used when needed for features you choose. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings. When you request a location label, Apple's geocoding service may process the location to return a place name. Reminders use local notifications and may show information on your lock screen according to your iPhone settings.

Apple may handle device backups, distribution, TestFlight feedback and diagnostics under your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policy. The app does not provide its own backup or sync service.

Sharing and support

Exporting creates files on your iPhone. The files leave Proof when you choose a recipient or another app in the share sheet. A PDF can include original attachments and verification data as well as visible pages. Review your selection before sharing. Copies held by recipients are outside our control.

If you contact admin@ecomagain.com, we receive the address and information you send. The built-in integrity support report contains check results and app version, without entry text or originals. Do not send sensitive record contents unless you choose to and they are needed for your support request.

Website, analytics and advertising

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes web connection and request information under its Privacy Statement. These pages add no analytics scripts, advertising trackers or cookies. Proof has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. We do not sell personal data or share it for targeted advertising.

Retention and your choices

Sealed entries cannot be edited or individually deleted inside Proof; corrections are appended to preserve the record. Removing the app removes its local app data. Exported copies, files shared with other apps and any device backups must be managed separately. We cannot recover a lost local record or remotely erase copies held on your device or by other people.

We keep support correspondence only for as long as needed to address the request, maintain necessary support records or meet legal obligations. Service providers apply their own retention policies to connection data.

Rights and lawful processing

For data we receive, we process support information to respond to your request and provide the service, and necessary operational information for our legitimate interests in security and reliable operation. We comply with applicable legal obligations. Permission-dependent features are controlled by your choices in the app and iOS.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of data we hold, or object to its processing. Contact admin@ecomagain.com. We do not hold a copy of your local record to access or change for you. You may also complain to your local data protection authority; in Latvia this is the Data State Inspectorate.

Children and international services

Proof is intended for adult record keepers, not for use by children. A parent may include information about children in their own local record. Include and share only information you are entitled to use. DigiCert, GitHub, Apple and email providers may process connection or support information outside your country under their published policies and applicable safeguards.

Security and changes

Proof uses iOS file protection and chained fingerprints to make changes detectable. No device or service can guarantee absolute security or prevent loss. Protect your device and review exports before sharing them. We will update the date on this page when this policy changes and provide additional notice when required.
