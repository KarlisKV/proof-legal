What Proof does

Proof helps you record events, attach originals, keep reminders and export a dated record. Entries are sealed on the device with linked fingerprints. Public timestamps are requested when a connection is available. Sealed entries are not edited; later corrections and additions remain separate.

A timestamp supports checking a fingerprint and time. It does not verify who entered information, whether an event happened or whether a claim is accurate. A signed export protects the exported file's integrity and time; it does not turn a failed entry into a verified one. Missing or changed original files can be omitted with an explanation in the export.
